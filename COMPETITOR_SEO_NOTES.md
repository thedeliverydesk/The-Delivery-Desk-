# Competitor and SEO positioning notes

Research date: 2026-05-27

## What larger consultants do well

- Accenture positions supply chain consulting around transformation, resilience, technology, leadership and measurable business impact.
- DHL Consulting uses the line between business consulting and logistics expertise, which is close to where The Delivery Desk should sit, but it is still connected to a major logistics group.
- McKinsey uses senior authority, problem framing, operational savings and strategic insight to make logistics advice feel board-level.

Useful references:

- https://www.accenture.com/gb-en/services/supply-chain
- https://dhl-consulting.com/
- https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/logistics/how-we-help-clients

## What parcel and carrier platforms do well

- SmartConsign, Shipmate, Scurri and similar platforms focus on carrier management, integrations, automation, marketplace links and multi-carrier control.
- Huxloe and Moov Parcel sell better carrier access, buying power, customer service and simplified parcel management.
- Same-day courier competitors lead with collection speed, vehicle availability, tracking, proof of delivery and instant quotes.

Useful references:

- https://www.smartconsign.io/
- https://www.shipmate.co.uk/multi-carrier-parcel-management
- https://huxloe.com/parcel-delivery/
- https://www.moovparcel.co.uk/
- https://courierexpert.co.uk/
- https://www.scurri.com/carrier-management-software/

## How The Delivery Desk can beat them organically

The Delivery Desk should not pretend to be a carrier, broker, 3PL, software platform or giant consultancy. The strongest positioning is:

"Independent delivery diagnosis and partner matching for UK businesses."

That gives the site a different angle from competitors:

- It can advise before selling a specific service.
- It can cover parcels, same-day, 2-man, storage, freight, sea freight and international without looking conflicted.
- It can target problem-led searches, not only service-led searches.
- It can build authority through practical issue guides, local pages and operational language.

## Content structure to grow

- Service pages: explain the service, who it fits and what to check.
- Issues and solutions pages: target problem-led searches and build subject matter authority.
- Local landing pages: target service plus location searches.
- Comparison pages later: carrier vs broker vs 3PL, same-day vs next-day, parcel carrier vs pallet network, fulfilment vs self-dispatch.
- Sector pages later: ecommerce, furniture, trade suppliers, manufacturers, wholesalers, retail chains, importers.

## Language to keep using

- missed collections
- collection windows
- claims and surcharges
- proof of delivery
- wrong vehicle type
- customs paperwork
- total landed cost
- devanning and onward delivery
- room-of-choice delivery
- failed drops

## Language to avoid

- seamless logistics
- tailored solutions
- unlock efficiency
- end-to-end excellence
- generic "we provide the best service" claims
