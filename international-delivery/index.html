Service overview

EU and international parcel and freight

EU and international delivery needs more than a cheap label. Customs, tracking, service level, paperwork and total landed cost all matter. We look at the job first, then help you find the right kind of delivery partner.

Find the right delivery solution

What matters

Make sure the service fits before you chase quotes.

This service is usually relevant for UK businesses sending parcels, pallets or freight into Europe and worldwide. The wrong international option can look fine at booking stage and then fail at customs, tracking or final delivery.

What we check

  • destination countries
  • parcel or freight profile
  • customs paperwork
  • transit time and tracking
  • duties, taxes and total landed cost

Service fit

Use this when the problem is more than just getting another quote.

Good fit when

  • EU parcel delivery, worldwide parcels, pallets or freight
  • customs paperwork or tracking is causing problems
  • you need service options checked by destination and goods type

Watch for

  • unclear commodity details
  • poor customs data
  • unexpected duties or taxes
  • tracking that stops after export

Useful details

  • Collection and delivery areas
  • Volume, timing, weight and size
  • Current supplier or process issues
  • Any access, customs, storage or handling notes
