# SEO and conversion notes

## Current offer

The site is positioned as an independent delivery and logistics matching service for UK businesses. It should not read like a carrier, freight forwarder or courier company. The strongest angle is: "tell us the job, we will work out the right kind of partner."

## Services covered

- Daily parcel collections
- Same-day delivery
- White glove 2-man delivery
- Storage and fulfilment
- EU and international parcel and freight
- Pallet and freight
- Retail supply chain support
- Sea freight and container logistics

## Page structure

The build now creates 1,152 local landing pages: 8 services across 144 UK towns and cities. The pages are not identical boilerplate. They combine service-specific copy, regional route context, local town/city naming, different checks and service warnings.

That is enough for an early SEO footprint, but the priority locations should still be hand-polished before serious paid traffic or link building. Best next targets are London, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Liverpool, Glasgow, Bristol, Cardiff and Belfast across the highest intent services.

## Conversion priorities

- Keep the lead form simple and qualification-led.
- Ask for service, location, volume and the main issue before a long message.
- Make the assistant push users into the form once it detects a likely service.
- Add Google Sheets and Genie routing before collecting real enquiries.
- Add call tracking, analytics and form conversion events before running ads.

## Writing guidance

Avoid over-polished phrases such as "tailored solutions", "seamless logistics" and "unlock efficiency". The copy should sound like an experienced operator: practical, direct and aware of what goes wrong in real delivery work.

Good language:

- "missed collections"
- "wrong vehicle type"
- "customs, devanning and onward delivery"
- "failed drops"
- "collection windows"
- "total landed cost"
- "room-of-choice delivery"

Avoid language that makes the site look like it owns all delivery services itself. The brand promise is independent matching and practical qualification.
