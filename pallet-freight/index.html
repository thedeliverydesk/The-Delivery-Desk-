Service overview

Pallet and freight

Pallet and freight work depends on access, timing, load type and the service promise. It needs a practical look before choosing a partner. We look at the job first, then match the enquiry with the right kind of delivery partner from our network.

Find the right delivery solution

What matters

Make sure the service fits before you chase quotes.

This service is usually relevant for manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors and suppliers moving heavier consignments. Freight problems often start with small details: poor access notes, wrong vehicle type or a delivery window the carrier cannot meet.

What we check

  • pallet size and weight
  • forklift or tail-lift access
  • delivery booking requirements
  • part-load versus network freight
  • damage and claims exposure

Service fit

Use this when the problem is more than just getting another quote.

Good fit when

  • pallets, oversized consignments or heavier business freight
  • tail-lift, forklift or booking-in details matter
  • damage risk, access and timing need checking before booking

Watch for

  • wrong pallet dimensions
  • tail-lift not specified
  • delivery booking ignored
  • poor access notes or no contact details

Useful details

  • Collection and delivery areas
  • Volume, timing, weight and size
  • Current supplier or process issues
  • Any access, customs, storage or handling notes

Example situations

Where pallet and freight needs proper qualification.

These examples show the kind of detail we check before matching an enquiry. Approved customer stories can be added once clients are happy for us to share them.

Example situation

Trade supplier

Pallets are reaching sites late or with access problems, so we would check tail-lift needs, booking-in, contact details and delivery restrictions.

Example situation

Manufacturer movement

Outbound loads vary in size, so the fit may be pallet network, part-load or dedicated freight depending on timing, weight and damage risk.

Example situation

Retail replenishment

Stock needs moving between warehouse and stores, so we would check delivery windows, unloading requirements and proof of delivery process.

Example situation

One-off project freight

A heavy or awkward consignment needs moving once, so the brief must cover dimensions, weight, access, equipment and realistic delivery timing.

Questions businesses ask

Pallet and freight questions.

Is pallet freight different from parcel delivery?

Yes. Pallet and freight services need more detail on access, weight, size, loading, unloading and booking requirements.

Can you help with one-off freight?

Yes. One-off and project movements can still be matched if the goods, route, access and timing are clear.
