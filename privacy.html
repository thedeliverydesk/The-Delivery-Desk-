Privacy notice

How enquiry details are used.

The Delivery Desk is a lead-matching site powered by SVMK. We use enquiry details to understand the delivery problem and arrange a relevant follow-up.

What information do we collect?

Business name, contact name, email, phone number, service type, location, volume, issue, partner application details, account profile details and anything else you choose to provide in a form.

Why do we use it?

We use the details to respond to the enquiry, understand the delivery requirement and, where relevant, introduce a suitable delivery, courier, freight, storage or logistics partner.

Who may receive it?

SVMK and The Delivery Desk may receive the enquiry. A suitable partner may receive the details if an introduction is needed to progress the request.

How long is it kept?

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond, manage any introduction and maintain a reasonable record of the request. Details can be corrected or removed on request unless we need to keep them for legal, accounting or legitimate business reasons.

How do you ask about your data?

Email andy@svmk.co.uk and ask for your details to be corrected or removed.
