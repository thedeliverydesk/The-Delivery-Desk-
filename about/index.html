Our position

We are not trying to make every job fit the same carrier.

Daily parcels, same-day work, white glove 2-man delivery, pallets, EU and international freight, storage, fulfilment and sea freight all fail for different reasons. A good match starts by asking what is moving, how urgent it is, what the customer expects and where the current process is leaking time or money.

That is why The Delivery Desk asks for the details before routing an enquiry. It protects the customer from irrelevant proposals and gives partner companies a clearer lead.

What this helps avoid

  • Choosing on headline price alone
  • Using a parcel service for bulky or fragile goods
  • Missing customs, access or booking requirements
  • Ignoring failed delivery and claims costs
  • Sending weak enquiries to the wrong partner
