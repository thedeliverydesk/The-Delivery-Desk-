Terms

Website terms and introduction notice.

The Delivery Desk helps businesses frame delivery and logistics enquiries and, where appropriate, introduces suitable partners.

Independent matching

We provide practical enquiry qualification and partner matching. We are not the carrier, courier, warehouse, freight forwarder or final service provider unless separately agreed in writing.

Partner introductions

Any partner introduction is based on the information provided by the business and the known partner fit at the time. The final service agreement, pricing, liability and service terms are agreed directly with the relevant provider.

Information accuracy

Businesses should provide accurate shipment, goods, customs, access, timing and handling information. Wrong or incomplete details can affect pricing, service availability and delivery performance.
