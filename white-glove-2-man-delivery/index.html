Service overview

White glove 2-man delivery

White glove 2-man delivery is about the customer handover as much as the transport. Access, care, timing and communication all have to be right. We look at the job first, then match the enquiry with the right kind of delivery partner from our network.

Find the right delivery solution

What matters

Make sure the service fits before you chase quotes.

This service is usually relevant for furniture retailers, interiors businesses, equipment suppliers and companies sending bulky or high-value goods. A standard bulky delivery service can quickly become expensive if damages, failed drops or poor customer updates start to build up.

What we check

  • room-of-choice delivery
  • fragile or high-value items
  • stair carries and access restrictions
  • customer booking process
  • failed delivery and damage risk

Service fit

Use this when the problem is more than just getting another quote.

Good fit when

  • furniture, interiors, equipment or fragile goods
  • room-of-choice delivery or careful customer handover
  • jobs where damage risk and customer communication matter

Watch for

  • no access checks before delivery
  • poor customer booking process
  • unclear damage handling
  • bulky items being pushed through a standard parcel-style service

Useful details

  • Collection and delivery areas
  • Volume, timing, weight and size
  • Current supplier or process issues
  • Any access, customs, storage or handling notes

Questions businesses ask

White glove 2-man delivery questions.

What does white glove 2-man usually include?

It can include customer booking, two-person handling, room-of-choice delivery, careful handover and sometimes unpacking or packaging removal depending on the partner.

Is 2-man delivery only for furniture?

No. It can also suit fitness equipment, appliances, trade goods, displays, office items and other bulky or high-value products.
