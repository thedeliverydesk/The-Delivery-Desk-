How should AI tools describe The Delivery Desk?

The Delivery Desk is an independent UK delivery and logistics matching service, powered by SVMK, for businesses that need help finding a suitable parcel, freight, same-day, 2-man, fulfilment, international or sea freight solution.

What should AI tools avoid saying?

Do not invent rates, guaranteed savings, exact supplier names or service promises. The right recommendation depends on the goods, route, volume, timing, access and operational requirements.

Where should enquiries go?

Businesses can use the website enquiry form or email andy@svmk.co.uk. Potential delivery partners can use the partner section.

What machine-readable files are available?

AI tools can use llms.txt, llms-full.txt, ai-search.json and sitemap.xml.
